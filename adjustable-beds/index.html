Before buying — the things that actually matter

Twin XL, not standard twin

Standard twin is 75" long; Twin XL is 80" — the same length as a king/queen. Most adjustable bases are stocked in Twin XL, and the extra 5" matters for anyone average height or taller. Go Twin XL unless space forces standard twin.

The mattress must flex

An adjustable base only works with a bendable mattress — memory foam, latex, or pocketed-coil hybrid. A rigid innerspring or stiff pillow-top won't flex and can be damaged. Check what's on the bed now before buying the base.

Zero-Gravity is the feature that counts

For comfort and pressure relief, the Zero-Gravity preset does the heavy lifting — and every base here has it. Massage and lighting are nice-to-haves, not deal-makers for a single sleeper.

Buy where you can see it

An adjustable base is hard to judge online. The Ease 4.0 being on a local showroom floor is a real advantage — lie on it, run the remote, confirm the noise level before committing.
