Open Questions Before You Call

  1. Do you have colors already selected, or do you want color consultation? Both PaintGreen and Procoat work with Benjamin Moore and Sherwin-Williams; if you want a color consultant included, ask upfront — some painters partner with designers, others don't.
  2. Interior only, or do you also want an exterior quote while they're there? Both do exterior. Could be convenient if exterior is on the horizon.
  3. Full house vs. specific rooms? Clarify scope early so the estimate is accurate. Procoat specifically is known for detailed scoping — reviewers mention the quote matched the final cost.
